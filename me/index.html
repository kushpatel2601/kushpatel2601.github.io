Problem Statement

📌 Official Problem Statement — Problem 9

Build an AI-driven urban transit optimization platform that makes public transport reliable and safe for female commuters using real-time data and automated anomaly detection. The platform should address overcrowding, safety risks, poor scheduling, and the lack of trustworthy information available to women who depend on public transit daily.

⚠️

Safety & Harassment

Women face daily safety risks in crowded buses and at poorly lit stops, with no automated alert or reporting mechanism.

📊

No Real-Time Data

Transit authorities lack live crowd density and anomaly data to make proactive decisions and prevent incidents.

🕐

Unreliable Scheduling

Poor schedule adherence and unpredictable routes force women to wait in unsafe conditions for extended periods.

2

Proposed Solution — SafeTransit AI

🚌 SafeTransit AI — Overview

SafeTransit AI is a unified, three-layer platform combining a commuter-facing mobile app, an operator-facing dashboard, and an AI anomaly engine working together in real time. Female commuters get safe route suggestions, crowd alerts, and one-touch SOS — while transit operators get predictive insights to improve fleet scheduling and incident response.

Data Flow — Step by Step

1

Multi-Source Data Collection

GPS traces from buses, IoT crowd sensors at stops, user-submitted safety reports via the app, CCTV feed metadata, historical crime datasets, and civic lighting maps are continuously ingested.

GPS Tracking IoT Sensors Crowd Reports
2

Real-Time Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

Incoming streams are cleaned, normalized, and enriched with time-of-day, day-of-week, and weather context. Geospatial clustering identifies high-density hotspots on the fly.

Apache Kafka Stream Processing Geofencing
3

AI Anomaly Detection & Safety Scoring

An LSTM-based model flags unusual crowd spikes and route deviations. A separate safety scoring model assigns a Safety Index (0–100) to each bus stop and route segment using crime, lighting, and incident history.

LSTM Model Safety Scoring Anomaly Flags
4

Intelligent Route & Alert Dispatch

Safe route recommendations are pushed to commuter apps in real time. Operators receive priority alerts for anomalies. High-severity incidents trigger automatic SOS routing to emergency contacts and transit control.

Push Alerts SOS Routing Operator Broadcast
5

Operator Action & Feedback Loop

Transit operators reroute buses, deploy extra vehicles, or coordinate with local police via the dashboard. All actions are logged and feed back into the model for continuous improvement.

Rerouting Model Retraining Audit Logs

Core Feature Modules

🗺️ SafeRoute Navigator

  • Recommends routes with highest safety scores
  • Avoids high-crime or poorly lit stops
  • Live crowd density indicators per bus
  • Integrates real-time bus ETA

🚨 Anomaly Alert Engine

  • LSTM detects sudden crowd spikes
  • Flags buses deviating from scheduled routes
  • Sends alerts to both commuter & operator
  • Severity classification (Low / High / Critical)

📊 Operator Intelligence Dashboard

  • Live fleet map with color-coded safety zones
  • Predictive demand heatmaps (next 30 mins)
  • Incident log with one-click response tools
  • Exportable analytics for route planning

🆘 Emergency Response Module

  • One-tap SOS from the commuter app
  • Auto-share live GPS to emergency contacts
  • Silent alert mode (no sound, no screen wake)
  • Direct integration with transit control room
